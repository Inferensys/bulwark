# Bulwark datasets

Generated by `bulwark datasets --markdown`. Do not edit by hand; edit `bulwark/data/registry.py`.

`Commercial`: the maintainers' reading of the dataset's published terms is that commercial use is permitted. This is not legal advice; check the licence yourself before relying on it. Every clip in the manifest carries this flag. The commercially clean retrain is the `configs/train/*-clean.yaml` chain: every stage trains only on clips with this flag (`commercial_only: true`) and refuses checkpoints or teacher features made without it (see [training.md](training.md)). It covers Bulwark's data, not the encoder's upstream pretraining.

`Release`: whether weights trained on the dataset may be published: `yes`, `no` or `unknown` (not yet determined). A `yes` or `no` rests on the dataset's terms, its owners' written permission or the maintainers' own dated decision, and Terms relied on says which ("Weights release"). `yes (maintainers' decision)` is Inferensys's decision, made on the dataset's research terms, that v0 weights trained on it may be published for non-commercial research; it is not a statement by the dataset's owners, and users must also follow the dataset's terms. A checkpoint is ready for release only when every dataset it was trained on is `yes`, and `bulwark release-check` prints the basis with its verdict.

`Kaggle` and `Azure`: whether a private copy may be kept on that third party's storage: a private Kaggle dataset, or the private Azure Blob container in the project's own subscription. Each provider is decided on its own. `yes` or `no` (the terms or signed agreement have been read and the maintainers have decided; Terms relied on quotes the clause or records the dated decision) or `unknown` (not decided yet). `infra/kaggle/push_data.sh` (Kaggle) and `infra/azure/upload.sh` (Azure Blob) refuse the shards, feature files and manifest rows of datasets that are not `yes` for their provider unless the operator names them (`ALLOW_UNCLEARED`), and `scripts/purge_cloud_copies.sh` deletes the shard blobs of a dataset that is not `yes` for Azure instead of replacing them.

`Labels`: the taxonomy labels (`bulwark/data/taxonomy.yaml`) the adapter writes as ground truth.
