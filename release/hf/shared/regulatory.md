Bulwark is not certified or type-approved for any use. It has not been tested against:

- the driver drowsiness and attention warning (DDAW) rules of the EU General Safety Regulation (GSR), [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1341](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2021/1341/oj/eng);
- the GSR's advanced driver distraction warning (ADDW) rules, [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2590](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2023/2590/oj/eng);
- the driver monitoring protocols of the European New Car Assessment Programme (Euro NCAP).

The DDAW rules measure drowsiness on the Karolinska Sleepiness Scale (KSS), which Bulwark does not estimate.

Other rules may apply to what you build with it:

- Using AI to monitor workers may be high-risk under the EU AI Act (Annex III, point 4).
- Face video is personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
